# Northstar Digital Preview

Fictional destination specification for **The Last Lightkeeper**.

## 1. Program media

The preview master MUST be a QuickTime MOV with H.264 video at 1920 by 1080
pixels and a frame rate of 24000/1001 fps.

## 2. Program audio

The preview master MUST contain exactly two stereo audio channels at 48000 Hz.
Integrated loudness MUST be between -21 LUFS and -17 LUFS. True peak MUST NOT
exceed -2 dBTP.

## 3. Captions

English preview captions MUST be supplied as one of SRT or WebVTT sidecar
formats.

## 4. Filename

The preview master filename MUST exactly equal
`the-last-lightkeeper-preview-v1.mov`.
